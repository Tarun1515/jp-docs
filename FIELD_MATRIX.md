>

> one it says **where** we ask, whether it is **compulsory**, and **why** we
> ask at all. The last column is yours.
>
> **How to use it:** go down the "Your decision" column. Write *keep*, *move*,

## The one thing we need you to understand first

There are **two moments** where we can ask a school for something:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Registration** | Before you have approved them. They are a stranger filling in a form. Every extra question here loses some schools who would have signed up. |
| **Profile** | After approval, once they are inside and motivated. Questions here cost almost nothing, and they can come back and finish later. |

The rule we have followed: **ask at registration only what you need in order to
decide whether to approve them.** Everything else belongs in the profile.

That is why we are proposing to move the principal's name and mobile out of
registration — you told us on the call that you do not use them to approve.

## B. 🔴 The website question — we need you to choose

You asked for **website to be compulsory**. We are flagging a contradiction
before building it, because it works against something else you have said.

**The case for compulsory:** a school with a website is easier to trust. It
tells a teacher this is a real, established place. It raises the quality of
what a job-seeker sees.

**The case against:** you have told us the portal is for schools in smaller
towns and villages too — that is who your sales team is reaching. **Most of
those schools do not have a website.** Not a bad one, not an old one. None.
If website is compulsory:

- they cannot finish registration at all, or
- they type `na`, `none`, `-`, or `facebook.com/ourschool`, and now the field
  is compulsory *and* full of rubbish — which is worse than optional, because
  you can no longer trust it.

**Our suggestion** — optional, but asked prominently, with a line that says why
it is worth adding. Schools that have one will fill it in; schools that do not
are not turned away at the door.

> ☐ **Compulsory** — a school without a website cannot register
> ☐ **Optional but prominent** (our suggestion) — asked clearly, never blocks
> ☐ **Optional, as today** — a quiet field at the bottom

## D. One school or several — how this works

On the form, a school chooses one of two things. Today that choice is a
technical word with no explanation, which is why nobody picks the right one.
We are replacing it with plain language:

> **Is this one school, or a group?**
>
> ☐ **One school** — we have a single campus at one address.
> ☐ **A group of schools** — we run more than one school or campus, and want to
> post jobs for each of them.

If they choose group, then **after you approve them**, their dashboard shows
*"Add your next school or campus"* where they can see it. Today a group can add
more schools, but the option is buried and nobody finds it — so groups register
once and never add the rest.

### And one question we could not answer ourselves

The Free plan gets **one job post**. One job post *how often*?

> ☐ **One per month** — a free school can post a new job every month, for ever.
> ☐ **One ever** — a free school posts one job, and must pay to post a second.

These are very different businesses and we cannot guess which you meant. The
first is a trial that never ends; the second is a trial that converts. We have
built the system so either works, and we need you to pick one.
